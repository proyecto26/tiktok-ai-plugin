# Ad copy for TikTok

Dated 2026-11-22. Character limits and button names change: **verify in Ads Manager's creative step.**

On TikTok the video carries the ad; the text is a caption under it. Write the hook first (spoken or on screen in
the first 2 seconds), then the caption, then the call to action.

## For each concept give

1. **Hook** (first line spoken or on screen, 2 seconds).
2. **Ad text** (the caption; keep it short, roughly 100 characters is the usual in-feed limit, verify; lead with the
   outcome, put the offer second).
3. **Call to action** button (Learn more, Shop now, Sign up, Download, Contact us, Order now...).
4. **Brief**: format (9:16), first frame, on-screen text, length (most winners are 9 to 30 seconds), sound plan,
   person on camera or not.
5. **Placement and destination.**

Lint the text with `../tiktok-content/scripts/caption-lint.ts` and check AI tells with
`../tiktok-content/scripts/ai-tells.ts`.

## Hook formulas (spoken or on screen)

| Formula | Example shape |
|---|---|
| Problem question | "Still spending [time] on [task]?" |
| Pattern break | "Stop [common habit]. Do this instead." |
| Result first | "[Number] [result] in [time], here is how." |
| Proof | "I tried [thing] for [days]. Here is what happened." |
| Objection | "You think [objection]. Here is why it is not true." |
| Identity | "If you are a [specific person], watch this." |

The identity hook is also a targeting signal for delivery: adding the niche word ("dental", "freelance designer")
to a winning ad is a cheap variant. Variants this close count as one concept, so keep volume coming from new
concepts, not from word swaps.

## Ad text formulas

**Problem, agitate, solve**
> [Problem]. [What it costs you]. [Product] fixes it in [time]. [CTA]

**Before, after, bridge**
> Before: [painful state]. After: [desired state]. [Product] is the bridge.

**Social proof lead**
> "[Specific result]", says [customer]. [What you do]. [CTA]

**Direct response**
> [Product]: [outcome] for [audience]. [Offer or deadline, only if real]. [CTA]

## Native checklist

- Looks like a post from a person, not a brand spot; captions burned in; sound on.
- Opens on the claim or the face, not on a logo.
- One idea per video; one call to action.
- A real offer or deadline only; no invented urgency.
- Claims the product can back up; no before and after promises that regulators treat as misleading.

## Testing priority

Hook first (it changes who stops), then the visual treatment, then the format, then the caption and button.
Change one thing at a time inside a concept, and never edit a performing ad: launch the variant beside it.
