# Audience targeting on TikTok

Dated 2026-11-22. Options, minimum sizes and rules change: **verify in Ads Manager.** Targeting is set by the user
in Ads Manager; this skill only recommends.

## Options

- **Demographics**: age, gender, location, language. TikTok skews younger, but do not assume the buyer's age; check
  the account's own audience in Studio first.
- **Interest and behaviour**: categories built from viewing and interaction (video interactions, creator
  interactions, hashtag interactions). Broad.
- **Device and connection**: operating system, device price band, connection type.
- **Custom audiences**: people who visited the site or took an event (pixel), engaged with your videos or profile,
  watched a share of your videos, followed you, or a customer list (file upload).
- **Lookalikes**: people similar to a custom audience. Quality follows the seed: use the best customers, not all
  visitors.
- **Automated audience**: let delivery find people from the creative and the optimisation event.

## Recommended approach

1. **Start broad** (the country, a wide age range) and let the creative qualify the audience. Narrow only when the
   data shows a segment that converts at a lower CPA.
2. **Test audience as a variable after creative**, not before: a losing creative hides a good audience.
3. **Retarget in layers**: video viewers and engagers get the proof ad; site visitors and add-to-cart get the offer;
   exclude purchasers from prospecting.
4. **Exclusions**: customers, recent converters, employees, and your retargeting audiences from cold campaigns.
5. **Size**: very small audiences saturate fast (frequency above 4 in `decision-system.md` is critical). Widen
   rather than rotate creative every few days.

## Research inputs from this plugin

- `analytics` -> `traffic_sources` and `search_terms`: where views come from and what people searched before
  finding the account. Search terms are usable keywords for hooks and captions.
- `tiktok-research`: what works in the niche (creators, sounds, hashtags). Adapt the structure, never the copy.
- Comments with questions on organic posts show buying intent and objections; answer the objection in the next ad.

## Region and policy

Some products and audiences (age-restricted categories, financial and health claims, minors) have special ad
policies. Check TikTok's ad policies for the category before recommending a creative or an audience.
